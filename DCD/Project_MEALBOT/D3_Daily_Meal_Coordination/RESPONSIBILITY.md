Mission

Turn residents’ stored preferences into one final meal plan for tomorrow.

D3 answers:

What will this Home eat tomorrow?

What D3 Owns

The daily meal poll.

Poll options.

Resident votes.

Poll opening and closing state.

Winner selection.

The final meal plan for a specific date.

What D3 Does Not Own

Creating Homes, Residents, or Cooks. That is D1.

Long-term food preferences. That is D2.

Sending WhatsApp/group/DM messages. That is future Notification Delivery.

Waking up at 11 PM. That is future scheduling/runtime infrastructure.

Cooking the food. That belongs to the Cook in the real world.

DailyMealPoll
- A DailyMealPoll is the business object that allows one Home to decide tomorrow's meal by collecting votes on a fixed set of meal choices.
- It represents the Home's process of deciding tomorrow's meal.

User Story line:
- The user has finished configuring the Home and is now using MealBot to make tomorrow's meal decision.
